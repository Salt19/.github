# SALT19

**Applied AI Systems Lab**

SALT19 builds and validates practical AI-enabled systems across autonomy, geospatial intelligence, unmanned systems, computer vision, sensor fusion, engineering automation, and agent infrastructure.

**Systems · Verification · Real Impact**

## What we work on

Our engineering portfolio includes:

- **EvoMind** — experimental cognitive architecture work focused on reasoning, memory, planning, evidence, self-modeling, and governed improvement.
- **AeroClear** — airspace intelligence and operational awareness tooling.
- **DroneMesh** — local photogrammetry, reconstruction, mapping, and 3D output workflows.
- **TACWING X** — local AI-assisted command, control, and operator-awareness tooling for supported unmanned aircraft ecosystems; currently under active development.
- **Agent Utility Grid** — infrastructure for discoverable agent-facing utilities and machine-consumable services.
- **Applied UAS analysis tools** — computer-vision, thermal, vegetation, and inspection workflows for field operations.

## Engineering principles

We favor systems that are:

- **Local-first where practical** — minimize unnecessary cloud dependency and preserve operator control.
- **Evidence-driven** — test claims, preserve artifacts, and distinguish demonstrated capability from planned capability.
- **Composable** — reuse proven components across products instead of rebuilding the same capability repeatedly.
- **Operationally grounded** — design for real workflows, field constraints, failure modes, and recovery.
- **Governed** — treat authority, security, provenance, and human control as engineering requirements.

## Working with SALT19
