# SALT19 Community Conduct

SALT19 expects technical collaboration to remain professional, evidence-based, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should:

- critique ideas, designs, code, tests, and evidence rather than attacking people;
- document disagreements clearly and support technical claims with evidence when practical;
- keep project channels relevant to the work;
- respect confidentiality, intellectual property, security boundaries, and customer restrictions;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or deliberate disruption;
- disclose conflicts of interest that materially affect technical review or project decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable in SALT19 project spaces:

- harassment or targeted abuse;
- doxxing or publication of private information;
- knowingly posting credentials, confidential data, or security-sensitive information;
- impersonation or misrepresentation of authority;
- malicious code, covert access mechanisms, or intentional sabotage;
- repeated disruption after a maintainer has asked that behavior to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect project integrity, security, or participants. Serious concerns may be reported privately to info@salt19.com.

Repository-specific conduct requirements may supplement this policy.
