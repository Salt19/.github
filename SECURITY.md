# SALT19 Security Policy

SALT19 treats security, credential protection, provenance, and controlled disclosure as engineering requirements.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do **not** open a public GitHub issue for a suspected vulnerability, exposed credential, unauthorized access path, data leak, or other security-sensitive finding.

Report security concerns privately to:

**info@salt19.com**

Include, when available:

- affected repository, component, version, commit, or deployment;
- a concise description of the issue and potential impact;
- reproduction steps or proof-of-concept details sufficient for validation;
- relevant logs, screenshots, or indicators;
- whether you believe credentials, customer data, or production systems are exposed;
- a safe way to contact you for follow-up.

Do not include live credentials, unnecessary customer data, or destructive payloads in the initial report.

## Handling expectations

SALT19 will triage reports based on severity, exploitability, affected systems, and evidence. Validation, remediation, disclosure timing, and any public advisory will be coordinated according to the circumstances of the issue.

Please avoid public disclosure until SALT19 has had a reasonable opportunity to validate and remediate the issue.

## Scope

Repository-specific security instructions take precedence over this organization-wide policy. Customer systems and third-party platforms may have separate reporting requirements that must also be followed.
